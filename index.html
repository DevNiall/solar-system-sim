    About this simulation

    This is an educational, stylized model of the solar system built with Three.js. It is designed to teach real astronomy concepts — relative orbital speeds, planet ordering, and comparative facts — while remaining visually legible in a single viewport.

    What is NOT physically accurate:

    • Planet sizes are scaled up (especially the smaller ones) so they're visible at all — the Sun would otherwise dwarf every planet on screen.
    • Orbital distances use a compressed non-linear scale so Neptune isn't kilometers away from Mercury on your screen.
    • Orbital speeds preserve relative ordering (inner planets orbit faster than outer ones, matching Kepler's laws) but are sped up dramatically and not in true proportion.
    • Axial tilts, orbital inclinations, and moon systems (besides Earth's Moon) are simplified or omitted.

    Facts about diameters, day/year lengths, and moons are drawn from real published astronomical data (NASA/JPL). Only the geometry of the visualization is exaggerated for teaching purposes.
